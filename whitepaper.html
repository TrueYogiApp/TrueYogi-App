3. The Dharma Loop (Core Mechanism)

  1. Step 1: Meditate → Earn
    Choose 1, 3, 13, 30, 60, 90, or 180-minute sessions
    Up to 3 sessions per day (540 minutes max)
    Rewarded tokens = total minutes selected × Y̐10
    Only rewarded after completing a 41-day streak
    (Example: 3 x 180 min daily = 540 min x Y̐10 = Y̐5,400 after 41 days)
  2. Step 2: Discipline of the Streak
    Users choose their daily meditation start time
    Must begin within ± 10 minutes
    Missed 1 session or day: must restart
  3. Step 3: Claim and Flow
    After 41 days, tokens are unlocked
    Users can hold, share, or sell
    Even if tokens are gone — the awareness remains
  4. Step 4: Dharma Buyback
    Project treasury or founder buys tokens back
    Bought tokens go back into reward pool
    Enables infinite regenerative loop
🕉 This is the Karmic Economy of the awakened world.
